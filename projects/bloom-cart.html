BloomCart

A witty design approach to cultivating conscious consumption.

Event
UDesignathon 2026 — 24-hour design sprint
Team
3 designers
Focus
Sustainability, conscious AI search, computer vision

Overview

Pitched at UDesignathon 2026, in a 24h design sprint with a team of 3. We designed Bloom Cart, a smart online cart that uses conscious AI search and computer vision to help users shop more sustainably as easily as possible.

The goal

Make better choices easier to see and compare.
